Privacy Policy

Chess Arena · Effective date: November 27, 2026

Chess Arena is an offline chess game. This policy explains what information the app handles when you use it.

Information the app uses

The app lets you enter player names to display during a local game. Names and game activity are used only within the app to provide gameplay. The app does not require an account.

Collection and sharing

Chess Arena does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Gameplay is local to your device. The app does not include advertising or analytics services.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the Chess Arena GitHub repository.
